# Project Development Timeline

This project was completed over **six days within the given one-week timeline**, following a structured workflow from **data understanding, cleaning and KPI preparation to sales analysis, dashboard development, business insights and final project preparation**.

### Day 1 — Data Understanding, Cleaning & KPI Preparation

The first day focused on understanding the restaurant sales dataset, validating the data and preparing the required calculations for analysis.

#### Data Understanding

* Studied the structure of the **4,838 restaurant sales records**.
* Reviewed the **10 available fields** covering orders, dates, tables, dish categories, dishes, quantities, prices, waiters, payment methods and customer ratings.
* Understood the relationship between **Quantity, Price and Revenue**.
* Identified the KPIs required for restaurant sales and performance analysis.

#### Data Cleaning & Validation

* Checked the dataset for duplicate records.
* Checked missing and blank values.
* Reviewed Order IDs for consistency.
* Standardized dish categories and text values.
* Trimmed unnecessary spaces.
* Handled missing waiter names as **"Not Recorded"**.
* Handled missing payment methods as **"Unknown"**.
* Validated date fields and formatting.
* Checked Table Number and Quantity values for validity.

#### Data Preparation

* Created a structured Excel Table.
* Prepared the calculation structure required for analysis.
* Created the **Revenue** calculation using:

`Revenue = Quantity × Price`

* Organized the workbook into structured analysis sections.

#### Day 1 Outcome

By the end of Day 1, the restaurant sales dataset had been **understood, cleaned, validated and prepared for detailed analysis**, with the required calculated fields ready.
